About the State

Uttarakhand is a state in northern India. It was formed in the year 2000 and is divided into the Garhwal and Kumaon regions.

The state is popular for its mountains, rivers, forests and temples. Dehradun is the capital city and is also an important educational centre.

Uttarakhand is known for

  1. The Himalayan mountains
  2. Ganga and other rivers
  3. Hill stations
  4. Wildlife and forests
  5. Religious and historical places

Did you know?

Uttarakhand is often called the "Devbhoomi", meaning the Land of Gods, because of its many important temples and pilgrimage sites.
