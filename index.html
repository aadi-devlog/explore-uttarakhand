Welcome to Uttarakhand

Uttarakhand is a beautiful state in northern India. It is known for the Himalayas, rivers, forests and peaceful hill towns.

Why visit Uttarakhand?

There are many reasons to visit Uttarakhand. The state has beautiful mountain views and many places connected with nature and culture.

  • Beautiful Himalayan scenery
  • Famous temples and pilgrimage places
  • Adventure activities and trekking
  • Peaceful hill stations
